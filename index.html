    Questions, answered

    Something else? Talk to us.

    Which platforms does Adside work with?

    The AI Agency runs Meta, Google, LinkedIn, Reddit and X ads end to end. The managed service also covers TikTok and Snapchat.

    Does it work for 5 brands or 200?

    Yes. There's no minimum and no per-seat pricing. You pay per brand, so your tenth brand costs the same as your hundredth.

    Can I use Adside as an agency?

    Yes. Pricing is per brand, and you can invite as many people as you want.

    Will Adside replace my team?

    No. It takes the repetitive work off their plate (building campaigns, resizing creative, pulling reports) so they can spend their time on strategy. If you don't have an ads team, the managed service gives you an expert who runs everything.

    How long does it take to get started?

    Most teams are live the same day. Connect your Meta, Google and LinkedIn accounts, add your brands, and Adside gets to work.
